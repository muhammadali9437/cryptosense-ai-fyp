# How to use CryptoSense AI

## Overview page
The top card is Sage's plain-language read of the chosen coin plus the market mood gauge. Below it are the live candle chart (15m, 1h, 4h, 1d), 24h high and low, RSI and volatility, the AI signals list (tap a signal to see why), the watchlist, and snapshots of whale moves and new listings.

## Portfolio page
Add the coins you own with the amount and the price you paid. CryptoSense shows total value, profit or loss, and an allocation ring. Buying more of a coin updates the average price automatically. Keeping any single coin under half of the portfolio reduces risk.

## Alerts page
Create alerts for a price rising above a level, falling below a level, or moving a lot in 24 hours. The server checks prices every 30 seconds and creates a notification when an alert fires. A fired alert switches off; switch it on again to re-arm it.

## Profit and loss limits page
Set a stop-loss (where you exit if a trade goes wrong) and a take-profit (where you lock in a gain) for each coin. CryptoSense alerts you when a limit is reached. It never sells anything for you.

## News, Whale watch and New listings pages
News shows headlines tagged bullish, bearish or neutral by the FinBERT sentiment model, with Sage's take. Whale watch shows large transfers and whether money is moving onto or off exchanges. New listings shows coins listed on Binance in the last 60 days with a risk rating.

## Academy and Ask Sage
The Academy teaches how markets move in short lessons. Ask Sage answers questions using live prices, the AI signals, your portfolio, the latest news and the Academy lessons, and shows the sources it used.

## Daily routine
Read Sage's summary and the mood on Overview, check the chart and AI signals for your coins, scan News and Whale watch for anything unusual, keep every coin in your portfolio protected with a stop-loss, and set alerts so you don't have to watch the screen.
